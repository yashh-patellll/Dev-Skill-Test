Why is alt text important?

Accessibility

Enables screen readers to describe images to users with visual impairments, making your content accessible to everyone.

SEO Benefits

Improves search engine optimization by helping search engines understand and index your images properly.

Backup Display

Provides text alternatives when images fail to load due to poor connections or disabled image loading.

Alt Text Examples

Poor Example ✗

"IUPUI Jaguars know how to have fun on the dance floor."

Too vague and doesn't describe the specific content of the image.

Good Example ✓

"Chancellor Paydar and a student dance on a colorful dance floor at IUPUI Birthday Bash."

Specific, descriptive, and captures the important details.

How to Write Effective Alt Text

  • Be specific and descriptive about the image content
  • Keep descriptions concise (10-15 words maximum)
  • Include text that appears in informative images
  • Skip alt text for purely decorative images using alt=""
  • Focus on relevant details that convey the image's purpose
